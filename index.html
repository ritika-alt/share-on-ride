Our Services

Connect with Co-Travellers

We help you find people going to the same destination, so you can share the ride, split the cost, and make new connections. Whether it's daily commute or outstation travel, ride together, save together.

Our smart matching system pairs you with co-travellers based on time, route, and preferences like gender or group rides. You can even view basic profile info and user ratings before confirming your ride.

Sharing rides isn’t just about saving money — it's also about reducing traffic, cutting emissions, and creating meaningful connections on the go. You might meet a fellow student, a colleague, or a new friend.

Let’s make commuting less stressful and more sustainable — one shared ride at a time.

Flexible Scheduling

Plan your rides based on your preferred time. Our system allows you to schedule your journey in advance so you never miss a ride. You can also search for existing ride plans by others.

Before confirming a ride, you can view the co-traveler’s profile details such as name, gender, ride history, and user ratings. This helps you make informed decisions about who you ride with.

In case of any issues during the ride, a support option is available directly through the app. Users can also report inappropriate behavior or flag concerns easily.

Our community relies on transparency, respect, and trust — and we’re here to uphold that, every mile of the way.

Safety & Verification

We prioritize your safety. Each user goes through a basic verification process. You can also select preferences like gender or group rides for a more comfortable experience.



You can view basic profiles of fellow passengers before confirming a ride, including their name, profile picture (if added), and previous ride ratings.

To make your journey more comfortable, you can also set preferences such as traveling with verified users only, same gender co-riders, or selecting private group rides.

Our support team is available 24/7 in case of any emergency, and users can report any suspicious activity with just a click. Your peace of mind is our top priority.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does ShareRide work?

ShareRide connects users traveling to the same destination so they can share a ride and split costs. Just enter your pickup, drop location, and travel date to find matching users.

Is ShareRide safe?

Yes! We offer verified profiles, gender preferences, and community feedback so you can choose trusted co-riders for a safe experience.

Can I schedule a ride in advance?

Absolutely. You can plan your ride ahead by selecting a future date, and we’ll show you matching travelers for that day.

Does ShareRide provide cars?

No, ShareRide does not provide vehicles. We only connect people willing to share their ride or looking for one. It's all about peer-to-peer connections.

Is it free to use ShareRide?

Yes! The platform is free. The cost of the ride is agreed upon between users sharing the ride.
